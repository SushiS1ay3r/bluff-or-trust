TRUST OR BLUFF

Summary:
Trust or Bluff is a high-stakes card game inspired by the anime Kakegurui. In this intense battle, players must navigate a delicate balance between honesty and deception, using their single precious bluff at the perfect moment. Every card played adds to a shared total, and one wrong move could push you over the edge—literally. Will you trust your opponent's claim, or call their bluff and risk the consequences?
This is not just a game of cards; it's a game of reading your opponent, managing risk, and knowing when to deceive and when to play it straight. The AI learns from your behavior—adapting its strategy to counter your playstyle over multiple rounds.

Overview:
Trust or Bluff is a card game where players try to keep a running total below 10 by playing cards with values 0 to 3. Each player can bluff once per game by playing a card face-down and claiming its value. The opponent must decide to trust or call the bluff. The AI opponent adapts to your style.

Players: 2 (Human vs AI)
Setup:
16 cards total, with values ranging from 0 to 3
Each player starts with 4 cards
Remaining cards form the draw pile
Goal: Avoid pushing the total above 9
Bluff Mechanic: Each player can bluff once per game unless they have gained more bluffs
Card Play: Face-up or face-down (if bluffing)
Calling Bluff/Trust: Opponent decides whether to trust or challenge a bluff
Game Play
Coin Flip decides who starts, alternating turns.
On your turn, place one card in the center (face-up or face-down if bluffing)
The card’s value is added to a running total
If you bluff, you declare the true or fake value of the card
The opponent decides to trust or call bluff when a face-down card is played.
The card is revealed immediately after the decision
The game ends immediately when the total exceeds 9. The player who caused the total to exceed 9 loses
If Players run out of cards before game total reaches 9, then each player should draw one additional card from the deck, then continue the game

Bluffing Rules
Each player can bluff once per game initially
Players can earn extra bluffs by:
Correctly trusting the opponent's bluff
Successfully bluffing and not being caught

Trust or Bluff Rules
If the opponent says Trust:
If the card’s value matches the claim, the opponent gains an extra bluff opportunity
If it doesn’t match, the bluffer chooses to either:
KEEP the card’s value added to the total, or
LOSE the card’s value from the total
If the opponent says Bluff:
If the claim is true, the bluffer chooses to KEEP or LOSE the card’s value from the total
If the claim is false, the opponent chooses to KEEP or LOSE the card’s value from the total

GAME PLAY

Start with a coin flip, winner will start the game, then players will alternate turns until winner is decided
Each player will start with 4 cards each and take turns placing a card down in the center, the value of each card will be added to a total
Game ends when the total exceed 9, whichever player that placed that card which pushed the total over, loses
Each player starts the game with the ability to bluff once
A bluff is the ability to place a card in the game facing down and stating the value of the card

The player can either bluff about the value of the card stating a “fake” value, or state the true value of the card--it is up to their discretion
A player can gain more bluffs:
If the player “trusts” their opponent correctly, the player gains a “bluff”
OR
If the player’s “bluff” is correct and the opponent guesses wrong, the player gains a “bluff”
If Player 1 places a card face down, then player 2 has the ability to call out “Bluff” or “Trust”, once a call is made the card is immediately revealed to both players
If Player 2 calls trust the card Player 1 placed, and the card IS the true value then Player 2 gains an additional chance to bluff, the card placed remains
If Player 2 calls trust the card Player 1 placed and the card is NOT the true value, then Player 1 can choose to either KEEP or LOSE the card from the game total
If Player 2 calls bluff and the card IS the claimed value then Player 1 can choose to either either KEEP or LOSE the card from the game total
If Player 2 calls bluff and the card is NOT the claimed value Player 2 can choose to either either KEEP or LOSE the card from the game total
If Player 2 calls bluff and the card placed by player 1 (who claimed it was 0) has a true value of 0, then player 1 can STEAL (add the card back into their hand) or choose to KEEP it on the table
If player 2 calls bluff and the card placed by player 1 has a true value of 0, but claimed otherwise, then player 2 can choose to KEEP or STEAL the card
STEAL: they player can add the card to their hand

After a bluff or trust is resolved the game continues to the next players turn
If Players run out of cards before the game total of 9 is exceeded, then each player should draw one additional card from the deck, then continue the game as is
The total can not go below zero, if losing a card could causes this, then the total remains 0.
